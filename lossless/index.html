Is it really lossless?

Drop in an audio file. It stays on your device: nothing is uploaded. The page looks for the frequency cut-off that MP3 and AAC encoders leave behind, and reads every metadata tag it can find.

These are heuristics. A clean result is strong evidence but not proof, and a low-pass filter can also come from mastering. Decoding happens in your browser, so ALAC and rarer formats depend on what it supports.
